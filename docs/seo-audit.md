## Cannibalization check

A **conflict** is two URLs aiming at the same query: an identical primary
keyword, or the same phrase written with interchangeable trade words
("builders" / "contractors", "cost" / "price"). That is a hard failure.

An **advisory** is two keywords that merely share vocabulary. A parent/child
modifier split — `/miami-pergolas/` on "pergolas Miami" and
`/pergolas/aluminum/` on "aluminum pergolas Miami" — will always show up here
and is the intended structure, not a problem. Read them, do not chase them.

**No conflicts.** No two URLs declare the same primary keyword, and no two
primary keywords reduce to the same query.
